How it works

From Instagram to your private gallery
in 3 simple steps.

No technical knowledge needed. If you can download a file and double-click it, you can use this.

1
Download your data from Instagram ⏱ 2 min setup
Go to Instagram.com on your computer and follow this path:
  1. More → Settings → Accounts Center
  2. Your information and permissions → Download your information
  3. Click Download or transfer information → pick your account → Some of your information
  4. Scroll to Saved items and check: Saved posts, Saved collections, Saved music
  5. Set format to HTML (not JSON), date range to All time → click Create files
Instagram will email you a download link. This usually takes 15–30 minutes.
2
Drop the app into your export folder No install
  1. Download and unzip the Instagram export file
  2. Navigate inside to: your_instagram_activity / saved
  3. Download this app from GitHub and place the instagram-saved-library folder inside saved
saved/
├── saved_posts.html
├── saved_collections.html
├── saved_music.html
└── instagram-saved-library/ ← app goes here
    └── run.bat
3
Launch and enjoy Double-click
Windows: Double-click run.bat — that's it.

Mac / Linux: Open a terminal in the folder and run python app.py

Your browser opens with your entire saved library as a visual gallery. Search, browse, watch.
No Python? Open public/index.html directly in your browser and drag-drop your export files.
